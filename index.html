03 — Technology & managed services

Your complete IT department — proactive, predictable, always on.

Managed IT, security and cloud, with the senior guidance to align technology with your business goals.

Managed IT Services

MSP

Your complete IT department — proactive, predictable and always available. We keep your technology running so your team can focus on the business.

  • Help desk & end-user support

    Responsive support for your staff by phone, email and remote session, with on-site assistance when needed.

  • Proactive monitoring & maintenance

    24/7 monitoring of servers, workstations and network devices to catch and resolve issues before they cause downtime.

  • Patch management

    Scheduled updates for operating systems and third-party applications to keep your environment secure and current.

  • Backup & disaster recovery

    Automated, verified backups with tested recovery plans so your data survives hardware failure, ransomware or human error.

  • Network & infrastructure management

    Design, deployment and management of your network, Wi-Fi, firewalls and endpoints.

  • Vendor & asset management

    Licensing, procurement and coordination with your ISPs and software vendors — one number to call for everything IT.

  • IT staff augmentation

    Flexible IT staffing from our expert talent pool for rapid, cost-effective deployment alongside your team.

Managed Security Services

MSSP

Round-the-clock protection across your endpoints, network, cloud and identities — from detection through to response.

  • 24/7 threat monitoring & detection

    Continuous security monitoring and alerting across your endpoints, network, cloud and identities.

  • Endpoint protection (EDR/MDR)

    Advanced endpoint detection and response to stop malware and ransomware before it spreads.

  • Vulnerability management

    Regular scanning, risk prioritisation and remediation of security weaknesses across your environment.

  • Email & identity security

    Anti-phishing, spam filtering and account-takeover protection for Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.

  • Security awareness training

    Training and simulated phishing campaigns that turn your staff into your first line of defence.

  • Incident response

    Rapid containment, investigation and recovery when incidents occur.

  • Compliance & risk advisory

    Guidance and reporting support for the frameworks and regulations relevant to your industry.

Cloud Services

AWS · Azure · GCP

  • Cloud migration

  • Cloud infrastructure & DevOps

  • Data & analytics

    Fully managed serverless analytics that turn your data into insight without the infrastructure burden.

  • Cloud security
